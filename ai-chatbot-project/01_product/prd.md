
### **Product Name**
**AI Sales/Consulting Chatbot Platform**
*(Initial phase targets academic admissions, with future expansion to e-commerce, sales, and customer support)*

### **Problem Statement**
Currently, businesses, educational institutions, and sellers face the following challenges:
* Manual and time-consuming responses to repetitive queries.
* Lack of support for customers messaging outside of working hours.
* Sellers wasting valuable time handling basic FAQs.
* Traditional chatbots are overly rigid and rule-based.
* Current chatbots respond like simple FAQ bots, lacking genuine consulting capabilities.
* Inability of chatbots to understand the customer's context or emotional state.
* Chatbots fail to proactively ask for clarification when information is missing.
* High risk of AI hallucination when lacking sufficient data.

### **Product Vision**
To build an intelligent AI chatbot system capable of:
* Consulting naturally, akin to a human agent.
* Performing context-aware reasoning.
* Dynamically selecting the optimal response strategy.
* Autonomously retrieving external data when necessary.
* Proactively asking follow-up questions to fill information gaps.
* Packaging responses into multiple natural, human-like messages.
* Seamlessly integrating across multiple channels like Facebook and Zalo.

### **Product Goal**
Transform the standard **FAQ Bot** into a proactive **AI Consulting Agent**.

### **Target Users**
**End Users:**
* Students
* Parents
* Customers / Shoppers
* Inbound leads from Facebook/Zalo

**Business Users:**
* Sellers
* Consultants
* Admission Staff
* SMEs (Small and Medium Enterprises)
* Universities

### **Core Value Proposition**
How we differentiate from traditional chatbots:

* **Traditional Bot:** Question → FAQ Answer
* **Our Product:** Question → Understand Intent → Assess Customer State → Select Consulting Skill → Retrieve Information → Execute Reasoning → Determine Response Strategy → Deliver Natural Conversational Response.

### **Core Features**
* **Feature 1: Multi-channel Chat Integration**
    * Facebook Messenger
    * Zalo OA
    * Website Live Chat (Future phase)
* **Feature 2: Intent Detection**
    * *Examples:* Inquiring about tuition fees, asking about specific majors, requesting product details, checking policies, needing in-depth consultation, requesting human support.
* **Feature 3: Skill Selection**
    * AI dynamically triggers the appropriate skill/tool: Major consultation, Upselling, Objection handling, FAQ support, Human handoff.
* **Feature 4: Dynamic Retrieval (RAG)**
    * Fetching real-time data from: FAQs, official documents, policy guidelines, product catalogs, and CRM (Future phase).
* **Feature 5: Adaptive Reasoning**
    * *Shallow:* Direct answers for simple FAQs.
    * *Medium:* Providing explanations or making suggestions.
    * *Deep:* Multi-step reasoning for complex consulting scenarios.
* **Feature 6: Natural Response Packaging**
    * Splitting standard AI output into human-like message chunks with simulated typing delays.
    * *Example:* "I understand." -> [Delay] -> "Here is the detailed information..." -> [Delay] -> "Please let me know if you need further clarification!"
* **Feature 7: Human Handoff (Future phase)**
    * Seamless routing to a human seller/consultant based on CRM triggers or user request.

### **Success Metrics**
**AI Metrics (System Performance):**
* Intent detection accuracy
* Retrieval precision (RAG accuracy)
* Hallucination rate (Target: ~0%)
* Response latency

**Business Metrics:**
* Reduction in seller workload (Time saved)
* Increase in average response speed
* Increase in lead-to-customer conversion rate
* Increase in user engagement time
